# MedSphere User Guide

Guide for clinicians, nurses, and patient portal users.

## Getting Started

### Clinicians & Staff

1. Open your organization's MedSphere URL (e.g., `https://doctor.medsphere.health`)
2. Sign in with your hospital-provided email and password
3. Complete MFA enrollment on first login (scan QR code with authenticator app)
4. Select your facility if prompted

### Patients

1. Open the patient portal: `https://portal.medsphere.health`
2. Register or sign in with your invitation email
3. Verify your identity if required by your provider

## Patient Portal

### Appointments

- **Book:** Select provider, date, and visit type; confirm insurance if applicable
- **Reschedule:** Open upcoming appointment → Change date/time (subject to availability)
- **Cancel:** Cancel at least 24 hours before visit to avoid fees (policy varies by facility)

### Lab Results

- Navigate to **Lab Results** to view finalized results
- Abnormal values are highlighted; tap a result for reference range details
- Results appear after your provider releases them (typically 1–3 business days)

### Messaging

- Send secure messages to your care team (if enabled by your organization)
- Do not use messaging for emergencies — call 911

### Telemedicine

When `feature.telemedicine` is enabled:
1. Open your scheduled video visit from **Appointments**
2. Allow camera and microphone permissions
3. Join the waiting room; your provider will admit you

## Doctor Portal

### Patient Chart

- Search patients by name, MRN, or date of birth
- Review allergies, medications, problems, and recent encounters
- Document encounters with structured templates

### Orders

- **Lab:** Select panel or individual tests; assign priority
- **Imaging:** Order studies with clinical indication
- **Prescriptions:** E-prescribe with interaction checking

### Appointments

- View daily schedule with patient context
- Check in patients and mark no-shows
- Block time for procedures or admin

## Nurse Portal

- Vitals entry and intake documentation
- Medication administration record (MAR)
- Task lists and handoff reports

## Security Tips

- Never share your password or MFA device
- Log out on shared workstations
- Report suspicious access to your IT department
- MedSphere sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity

## Accessibility

MedSphere portals support:
- Keyboard navigation
- Screen reader compatible labels
- High contrast mode (browser/OS setting)

## Getting Help

- **Technical issues:** Contact your hospital IT help desk
- **Clinical questions:** Contact your provider's office directly
- **Portal access:** Use "Forgot password" or request a new invitation from registration

## Mobile Use

Portals are responsive and work on modern mobile browsers. A dedicated mobile app may be available depending on your organization's configuration.
